Field manual

Controls & Tactics

Read this once. It will keep your squad alive.

Touch controls

  • Tap a soldier card to select. Long-press a card (or press ALL) to add to the selection.
  • Tap the battlefield to issue the armed order at that point.
  • Drag to pan the camera. Two fingers to pinch-zoom.
  • Order buttons arm a mode; tap the world to execute. Tap MOVE to return to default.
  • QUEUE lets you stack up to four orders per soldier.

Desktop controls

  • Space tactical pause — orders still work while paused.
  • 1–6 select soldier, A select all, Shift+click add to selection.
  • Q queue, R reload, C cover, O overwatch, F focus, X suppress, H hold, T retreat, G regroup.
  • Wheel zoom, arrows pan, Esc pause.

Tactical doctrine

  • Elevation is life. High ground sees over low cover and shoots down into it.
  • Crouch behind cover. The TAKE COVER order puts a soldier in the protected slot; standing behind sandbags is not cover.
  • Suppress, then flank. Machine-gun fire pins enemies; use the pinned window to move assault troops.
  • Watch your lanes. Soldiers will not fire through a friendly body. Spread the formation.
  • Medics win campaigns. A downed soldier can be revived for 45 seconds. After that, they are gone for good.

Systems

  • Ammunition is tracked per magazine and reserve. Empty weapons fall back to a sidearm.
  • Cover degrades. Sustained fire destroys sandbags, crates, containers and fuel barrels. Barrels explode.
  • Wounds persist. Injured soldiers sit out operations; deaths are permanent and recorded in the memorial.
  • Enemies are not omniscient. They react to sight, gunfire and explosions, and share last-known positions.
Difficulty is honest: smarter enemies, not bullet sponges.
